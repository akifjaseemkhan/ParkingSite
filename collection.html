Return journey

Landed. Now let's
get you home.

Here's exactly what to do the moment your wheels touch the runway.

1

Confirm you've landed

Tap the link in your confirmation text, or call us — either starts your car moving from the compound straight away.

2

Clear the terminal as normal

Collect luggage and pass through customs — no need to rush, your car is timed against your actual flight data, not your text.

3

Head to arrivals pick-up

Your Wingate driver will be waiting with your car at the designated arrivals point for your terminal.

4

Quick check, then go

A final look over the car together against your handover report, and you're straight back on the road.
