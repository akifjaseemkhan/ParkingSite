Privacy Policy

1. What we collect 2. How we use it 3. Who we share it with 4. How long we keep it 5. Cookies 6. Your rights 7. Contact us

Last updated: 1 July 2026

Wingate Parking Ltd is committed to protecting your personal data in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. What we collect

When you make a booking or contact us, we collect: your name, email address, phone number, vehicle registration, flight details, and payment information (processed securely by our payment provider — we do not store full card details).

2. How we use it

We use your data to fulfil your booking, communicate about your journey (including flight tracking and collection updates), process payments, and respond to enquiries. With your consent, we may also send occasional travel tips by email — you can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Who we share it with

We share limited data with trusted partners strictly necessary to deliver our service — including our payment processor and, where relevant, the airport operator for access purposes. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. How long we keep it

Booking and vehicle handover records are retained for 7 years to meet insurance and accounting obligations. Marketing preferences are retained until you withdraw consent.

5. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to operate correctly, and optional analytics cookies to help us understand how the site is used. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings at any time.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Contact us

For any privacy-related question, email privacy@wingateparking.co.uk or write to Wingate Parking Ltd, Unit 4, Colnbrook Business Park, Nr Heathrow Airport, Slough SL3 0FG. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
