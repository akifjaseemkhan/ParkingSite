Meet & Greet

Pull up at departures, hand your keys to a Wingate driver, and walk straight in. It's the closest thing to airport parking having no downside at all.

  • No shuttle bus, no walk with luggage — a two-minute kerbside handover
  • Ideal for early departures, families and anyone travelling heavy
  • Return collection at arrivals, timed to your actual landing
  • Digital handover report, signed by you and your driver
  • Fully accessible for travellers with reduced mobility — no transfer bus, ever

Park & Ride

Leave your car at our compound a short hop from the terminal and take a frequent, direct shuttle the rest of the way. Full security, without the meet & greet premium.

  • Shuttles run every 10–15 minutes, 24 hours a day
  • Same gated, ANPR-controlled compound as Meet & Greet
  • Free transfer of luggage-friendly shuttles with low-step access
  • The lowest price per day across our entire range
