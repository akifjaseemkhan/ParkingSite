The Wingate way

Airport parking, reconsidered.

Most parking is an afterthought bolted onto a booking flow. We built Wingate the other way round — starting with the ten minutes either side of a flight that actually cause people stress, and designing those away.

You drive to the terminal, hand your keys to a uniformed member of our team, and walk in. Your car goes to a secured, monitored compound nearby. When you land, we're already bringing it back to you.

Vehicle care

We treat every car like it's the only one on site.

Each compound is mapped, camera-covered and access-controlled — every vehicle movement logged from the moment it arrives to the moment it leaves.

  • Digital condition report at handover and collection
  • Keys held in a coded, access-logged cabinet — never left in the car
  • Minimal mileage policy — your car only moves when it needs to
  • On-site team, day and night, for the full duration of your trip
