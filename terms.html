1. The agreement

These terms govern every booking made with Wingate Parking Ltd ("Wingate", "we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (Company No. 09123456). By completing a booking, you agree to be bound by these terms in full.

2. Booking & payment

Bookings are made by telephone and confirmed once full payment has been taken, with a confirmation sent to your email address for your records. The price agreed on the call is the total price payable, inclusive of the service selected and the standard £13 airport levy charged each way — no further charges are added at drop-off or collection unless you request an additional service on the day.

You are responsible for ensuring the details provided at booking — dates, times, flight number and vehicle registration — are accurate. Please contact us as soon as possible if any of these change.

3. Vehicle handover

At drop-off, you and your Wingate driver will complete a joint digital condition report recording the visible state of your vehicle, mileage, and fuel/charge level. Both parties retain a copy. This report is the reference point for the condition of your vehicle at handover and again at collection.

Keys are held in an access-logged cabinet for the duration of your booking and are only released to the registered account holder or an authorised nominee confirmed in advance.

4. Insurance & liability

Every vehicle in Wingate's care is covered by £10,000,000 combined goods-in-transit and public liability insurance from the point of handover to the point of collection. This covers loss or damage directly caused by our negligence while your vehicle is in our custody.

Wingate is not liable for mechanical faults, pre-existing damage recorded on the handover report, or loss of personal belongings left in the vehicle. We recommend removing valuables before drop-off.

5. Cancellations & changes

Bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled drop-off time, with a full refund issued to the original payment method within 5 working days. Cancellations inside 24 hours are considered on a case-by-case basis — please call us.

Date and time amendments are free of charge, subject to availability, and can be made any number of times before your drop-off slot.

6. Vehicle condition & conduct

Vehicles must be roadworthy, taxed, and insured for the duration of the booking. Wingate reserves the right to decline a vehicle at drop-off if it is deemed unsafe to move, in which case a full refund will be issued.

7. Delays & force majeure

Flight delays tracked automatically via your provided flight number do not incur additional charges. Wingate is not liable for delays caused by circumstances outside our reasonable control, including but not limited to severe weather, airport closures, or air traffic control action.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and any disputes will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
